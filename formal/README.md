# OpGate Rocq proofs

This directory holds the machine-checked model: the gate, the memory model of a launch, the step check, and the end-to-end theorem `tenant_isolation`. The library is `OpGate` (`-Q . OpGate`). Files are grouped by component and built in this order:

| Group | Files | What they prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gate | `GateIsa.v`, `GateForm.v`, `GateDomain.v`, `GateMeaning.v`, `GateSoundRange.v`, `GateSoundNorm.v`, `GateSoundJoin.v`, `GateSoundRefine.v`, `GateSoundTransfer.v`, `GateSoundOp.v`, `GateSoundLoad.v`, `GateSoundAccess.v`, `GateSoundLocal.v`, `GateThread.v`, `GateFront.v`, `GateParse.v`, `GateZero.v` | `verify_text` accepts a module only if every entry kernel's every access, in every execution of every thread block, stays in the region its binding allows, and every value stored to an index output lies in its range |
| Catalog | `CatalogStep.v` | `check_step` accepts a step only if the buffer lifecycle invariants hold from one step to the next |
| Store | `GatePrologue.v`, `StoreLaunch.v`, `StoreLink.v`, `StoreModel.v` | the launch facts hold of what a launch puts in the registers; the zeroing prologue stores zero over the shared variables before a thread reads them; a launch keeps every byte it may not write and keeps two runs in agreement on the bytes it may read, from any two shared memories; every run keeps every checked buffer in its range |
| Participation | `GateParticipation.v` | `verify_certificate_participation`: every entry that the deployed check accepts also passes the participation check of its barriers |
| Pipeline | `PipelineIsolation.v`, `PipelineGrants.v` | `tenant_isolation`: what a tenant observes after a pipeline depends only on what it observed before and on its own steps; `tenant_declassification`: with grants, it depends only on these and on the grants to it, with each granted buffer's record and bytes, and their ends |

The paper names each result by what it states; the Rocq names are:

| the participation theorem | `verify_certificate_participation` | `GateParticipation.v` |
| soundness of the termination check | `term_check_sound` | `GateTerm.v` |
| soundness of the shared-value route | `shared_check_sound` | `GateShared.v` |
| the frame lemma | `launch_frame` | `StoreLaunch.v` |
| the noninterference lemma | `launch_ni` | `StoreLaunch.v` |
| the prologue lemma | `prologue_run` | `GatePrologue.v` |
| the link lemma | `link_sound` | `StoreLink.v` |
| the invariant lemma | `run_steps_ok` | `StoreModel.v` |

Each file starts with a comment that states what it proves and what it trusts.

Build with `make -C formal build` (Coq 8.15.0, about 45 seconds from scratch); `make -C formal check` builds from scratch and removes the build afterwards if there was none.

The Gate files hold the checker and its soundness proofs. The checker knows spans with a direction (read, write, or read-write, which alone allows atomics), index inputs, and size symbols: extents and index ranges are forms over size registers, each fixed at launch with the range `[minimum, 2^31 - 1]` that the binding states (design decision 15). It has the launch geometry, the idioms of design section 5.5, and rules R1 (guards against a symbol, on the compared register's form and on its atom), R3 (index loads add the declared range as constraints), R4 (masks: a constant operand, or the relation to an exact operand such as `capacity - 1`), R5 (register steps), R6 (joins that keep shared terms, constraints of identical terms, and a constraint that follows from the other edge through its copies and normal forms), R7 (the check projects constraints onto an address by Fourier-Motzkin elimination), and R8 (local memory: frames, cells, and stores only inside a live variable, `GateSoundLocal.v` and the rule's section of `GateSoundTransfer.v`), each with its soundness lemma; design decisions 14 to 16 list them. Milestone 10 adds, each with its soundness lemma (design decision 24): `or.pred` (`IOrp` and the fact `FNeg`), the signed reading of a copied register, `or` of bits below an alignment, buffer bases at 256 bytes, stored values read signed or unsigned, dynamic shared memory (`b_shared`), R3 under a guard, joined with the old value and kept under a one-bit guard through its value (`guarded_index_load`, `guarded_cons`), constraints moved through a `selp` of two registers, or of a register and a constant (`sel_cons`, `const_cons`), `xor.pred` (`IXorp`), `xor.b32` (`BXor`, `xor_fact`), a register's high and low bits in a range (`low_bits` in `plain_range`, `plain_range_sound` in `GateSoundRange.v`), masks to a register's width, and a guard's bound on a bit-field atom as a constraint. For the Numba front end's `range` loop (design decision 25) it adds `abs.s<w>` as the larger of a value and its negation, a copy's constraints and range as the copy's own, a comparison with a constant on the compared register's atom when a constraint mentions that atom (`relate_atom`), the exact forms of registers as equalities over canonical atoms in a check (`form_eq_with`, `canon_cons`), a join's constraints read through the copies of one side, and the order's reading of a state's constraints through its copies (`cons_holds`), a loop register that a constraint mentions as the atom that the join's other stepping registers are read through, the elimination of an atom through an equality that has it (`fm_step`), and, for Triton, a signed shared load narrower than its register, a shared load at a variable, a load of a size parameter's low bits, and the signed range of a sign extension (design decision 26). The shared and local memory windows are in `GateFront.v` and `GateSoundAccess.v`. A shared memory load gives any value in the one-thread semantics, so `check_block_sound` follows from the theorem for one thread, and no block model with barriers is needed. The lowering requires that each entry kernel begins with the zeroing prologue of its shared and local memory (`zeroing_check` in `GateFront.v`) and that an instruction may read a register only after an instruction on every path writes it (`defined_check`), design decision 46. A dynamic shared variable whose bytes depend on the sizes is cleared by a loop whose count the prologue computes from the size parameters, and the lowered loops must have the form of their plan (`zero_program_check`); `GateZero.v` proves `accepted_zeroing`, that in every accepted kernel each thread stores zero at each of its elements of each such variable before it leaves the loops, and `zero_element_thread`, that each element has a thread of the block, so the threads of a block store every byte of the launch's dynamic shared memory before the barrier (design decision 49); both depend on no axioms. `kernel_safe`, the conclusion of `check_block_sound`, states for every instruction that a thread runs that its access lies in its region, that a store to an index output writes one whole element whose value lies in the range (`index_write` in `GateMeaning.v`, `in_range_sound` in `GateSoundAccess.v`), that every global, shared, and local access is aligned (`aligned_of_sound` in `GateSoundLocal.v`), and that every access to shared or local memory lies inside a variable of that memory (`private_in` in `GateMeaning.v`, `private_of_sound` in `GateSoundLocal.v`; design decision 20), given the launch fact that no buffer lies in a window (`lk_apart`). A trap, a breakpoint, a call of `__assertfail`, and behavior that PTX leaves undefined are a store to address 0 under the instruction's guard (`never_under` in `GateFront.v`), which no region contains, so an accepted kernel runs none of them (design decision 17). After `verify_ptx_sound`, `GateFront.v` has the diagnosis that names the failed check and the PTX instruction of a refusal; it decides nothing. `text_diagnosis` in `GateParse.v` gives the reason of a refused certificate and the first `LISTED` accesses that its walk refuses, for the guard pass, from one walk of each case that stops after the last listed access, and decides nothing either. `check_text_certificate_ok` is the check of the certificate (`check_front_ok`) on the front end's result of each entry kernel (`text_front front`, `check_text_front`); the gate's query computes that result with `front_program` outside the kernel and has the kernel check that it equals `text_front front`, so the proposal and the check read one result of the front end. The checked walk of a block (`checked_walk` with the plan `case_plan` in `GateDomain.v`) keeps, after every 16th instruction except the last, only the facts of the registers live after it, of the registers that the cases of the block's successors name, of the fixed registers, and of the registers these depend on; `trim_after_sound` (`GateMeaning.v`) holds for every choice of the kept registers, and `run_inv` (`GateThread.v`) states the run invariant for the walk of every plan of a block, with the trims of the check or without trims, which the termination check reads. `check_block_sound` and `verify_ptx_sound` depend on no axioms, and `verify_text_sound` only on the primitive-integer and primitive-array operations.

`CatalogStep.v` (milestone 7) defines `check_step`. A buffer's id is its tenant and a serial number (`bid`), and each allocation lies in its tenant's reservation, which `Reserve` makes apart from every other one and `Unreserve` ends once the tenant holds no live buffer (decision 52). The Python catalog transcribes it and `tests/formal` compares with it on generated steps, and proves `check_step_inv` (every buffer in the pool at a multiple of 256 bytes, with its own id, disjoint from the others; every grant on a live buffer of another owner) and `check_launch_sound` (the facts of each argument of an accepted launch); both depend on no axioms. A write carries its bytes, and the check computes the range of checked data from them.

The Store and Pipeline files (milestone 8, design decision 21) give the chain from the gate to isolation, and none of their theorems depends on an axiom:

- `GatePrologue.v`: `prologue_of` reads the lowered zeroing prologue of a kernel with shared variables off its program (the thread's index, the loop that clears dynamic shared memory, the rounds of stores of the other shared variables, and the barrier), and `check_entry_certificate` in `GateParse.v` requires it (`prologue_ok`). `prologue_run`: in the one-thread semantics, each thread stores zero over its share of every shared variable, reads no memory, and reaches the barrier (design decision 50).
- `StoreLaunch.v`: a launch as the threads of a grid over byte memory in the order of a schedule: global memory, a shared memory for each block, which starts with whatever the launches before it left there, and a local memory for each thread, which starts with any contents; the threads of a block wait at the prologue's barrier until each has arrived; oracles, functions of the bytes of the launch's own shared and local variables, give what the model does not compute. `launch_inv` keeps an invariant of memory from which each thread's launch facts follow; `launch_frame` is the frame of one launch; `launch_ni` and `launch_ni0` are its noninterference, from any two shared memories and from local memories that agree on the bytes of the local variables, for a kernel with shared variables and for one without.
- `StoreLink.v`: `link_sound`, the launch facts that `kernel_safe` assumes hold of the values the launch of an accepted operator puts in its registers; a register that a launch value does not fill holds 0, which the gate's check that registers are written before they are read keeps out of every run. The link is the per-kernel check `link_ok` in `GateFront.v`, which `verify_ptx` includes. `region_buffer` and `indices_keep` place every allowed access in a buffer argument and keep every index buffer in its range.
- `StoreModel.v`: worlds of a catalog and memory, the effects of each accepted action, and runs of steps; a kernel runs if the checks of a guarded launch hold, from whatever shared memory its blocks start with, and changes no byte otherwise. `run_steps_ok` keeps the catalog invariant and the status invariant, every element of a checked buffer in its range. The kernel table holds the program and facts of each verified operator: `verified_of_accepted` gives the model's `verified` premise from the gate's acceptance of each operator's text with its certificate (`check_text_certificate_ok`, what the service runs), on the primitive-integer and primitive-array operations only.
- `PipelineIsolation.v`: `tenant_isolation`, for two runs whose steps of tenant A are the same, from worlds whose own parts of A (`own`: A's buffers, the grants on them, A's freed regions, and A's reservation) are equal and whose memories agree on what A observes, when A's launches take the same hardware choices, A holds its reservation through both runs, and no buffer of another tenant is granted to A: the other tenants' steps, their reservations and data, and the order in which their steps interleave with A's may differ. `own_decision_same`: two catalogs with the same own part of A decide a step of A alike. `tenant_isolation_same_steps`: two runs of the same steps of every tenant from the same catalog, without the reservation's premises. The invariant `res_inv` keeps the reservations of two tenants apart and each buffer and freed region in its owner's reservation, with an id of its owner (decision 52).
- `PipelineGrants.v`: `tenant_declassification` drops the premises that A receives no grant. A's observations are a list of events: A's steps with their hardware choices, each grant to A with the granted buffer's record (id, offset, size, status) and its bytes at the grant, and each end of such a grant by the owner's free (`observe_steps`, which `observe_steps_run` and `run_observe_steps` relate to `run_steps`). Two runs with the same observations of A, from worlds with equal own parts of A, equal received grants (`received`), distinct grants, and memories that agree on what A observes, end with equal own parts, equal received grants, and memories that agree on what A observes. `check_step_grants_nodup` keeps the grants distinct.
- `GateParticipation.v`: `front_participation` states the route that the front end's participation check (`barrier_boundary` in `front_core`) takes for an accepted kernel: no waiting site, the straight-line case, `single_phase_safe` for one waiting site, or `front_events_safe` or `front_shared_safe` for more; `verify_certificate_participation` adds it to the conclusion of `verify_certificate_sound`.

The trusted facts of the hardware and the platform are `hardware_ok` in `StoreModel.v` (each thread's indices lie in the block and the grid, the platform's values are the same for the threads of a block, and every thread index of a block is a thread of the launch) and `platform_ok` in `StoreLink.v` (the windows and the variable addresses, with the two windows apart); the trusted fact of the prologue's local stores is `local_start` in `StoreModel.v` (a kernel that declares local variables starts with zeroed local memory; a kernel without starts from any contents, since the gate refuses every access to local memory then).
